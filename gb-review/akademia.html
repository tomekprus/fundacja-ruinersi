Workshops

Knowledge that helps you act.

Each day focuses on a specific issue found in old houses. We show you what to do, what not to do and what to look out for, and the sessions are led by people who work with historic buildings in practice.

Workshop venueVillage Hall
Radogoszcz 62
Workshop 1 · 26 September 2026 · 10:00–16:00

First steps in an old house

From purchase to the first assessment of the building: safe clearing, formalities, opening up building fabric, and recognising water, damp and visible signs of biological damage to timber.

Session 1 · Responsible renovation

  • From purchase to renovation — where to start and in what order to act.
  • Clearing the site and building — what can safely be done at the beginning.
  • Local planning rules and formalities — what to check before work begins.
  • Protection status and the role of heritage authorities — when work needs to be agreed and with whom.

Session 2 · Assessing technical condition

  • Water and damp — how to recognise early warning signs.
  • Smell — what it can tell you about the condition of an old building.
  • Biological damage to timber — visible signs to look for.
  • Technical condition assessment — how to use it to plan the next steps.

Practical information

Travel and accommodation

The workshops take place at the registered office of Fundacja Ruinersi na Dolnym Śląsku at Radogoszcz 67A, 59-800 Lubań.

By train

You can travel by train to Lubań Śląski from several directions, including Wrocław, Legnica, Bolesławiec, Jelenia Góra and Görlitz.

If you are arriving by train and cannot get from the station to the workshop venue, let us know in advance — where possible, one of our volunteers will pick you up at Lubań station and take you to Radogoszcz.

By car

The easiest option is to enter the exact address into your sat-nav: Village Hall, Radogoszcz 62. From Lubań, head towards Leśna and then Radogoszcz.

Parking will be available on site. Please follow the volunteers’ directions.

Where to stay?

If you are travelling from further away and want to stay in the area for the whole weekend, we recommend these friendly local guesthouses:

Dom pod Mandragorąul. Nadrzeczna 15, 59-850 Świeradów-Zdrój
Agroturystyka „Pradawna”Młyńsko 2, 59-620

It is worth booking in advance. The Foundation does not arrange accommodation.

Walks

Reading architecture in the landscape.

Walks led with Gildia Przewodników Sudeckich show timber architecture in its natural context: local history, landscape, tourism and everyday use.

20 Sep 2026 · 11:00 · Karkonosze route · Karpacz · approx. 4–5 hours

Karpacz and the area around Wang Church

Start: 11:00 at Wang Church. The walk is led by Gracjan Kieljański from Gildia Przewodników Sudeckich. We will look at Karpacz’s timber architecture, its materials and structures, and its place in the mountain landscape.

Buildings we will pay particular attention to: Wang Church, the former Japanese Embassy in Karpacz and the Museum of Sport and Tourism.

19 Sep 2026 · 11:00 · approx. 4–5 hours

Bóbr Valley: Siedlęcin → Wleń → Bystrzyca

Start at the Ducal Tower in Siedlęcin. We travel between the stops by car. The route is led by Katarzyna Kieljańska from Gildia Przewodników Sudeckich. Along the way we will look at different examples of historic architecture in the Bóbr Valley and how they relate to the local landscape.

Buildings we will pay particular attention to: the Ducal Tower in Siedlęcin and the ‘NiederHof’ granary in Bystrzyca.

Volunteering

Would you like to help with the Ruinersi Academy?

To prepare and run the Academy, we need people who can help with organisation and practical tasks. You do not need to be a building expert — willingness, reliability and readiness to work together matter most.

I want to volunteer with Ruinersi

How can you help?

  • Prepare spaces and materials before workshops and walks.
  • Support participants and help with organisation on site.
  • Support workshop leaders and experts during events.
  • Document events — photographs, reports and materials.
  • Information and promotion related to the Academy.
