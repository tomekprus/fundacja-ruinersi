Wspólnie dla dziedzictwa

As part of the Narodowy Instytut Dziedzictwa programme, the Foundation runs the Ruinersi Academy — a practical education series for owners of historic buildings. We focus on informed renovation planning, recognising risks, traditional materials and techniques, and making decisions that preserve the value and character of old houses.

Znaki szczególne Dolnego Śląska. House, craft, community

A project bringing together regional knowledge about Umgebinde houses, Sudeten cottages, timber framing, wood, clay and traditional infill.

About the programme

Current

17th Dary Lasu Festival

On 26 September 2026 in Lwówek Śląski, together with the Lwówek Śląski Forest District, we are co-creating the 17th Dary Lasu Festival under the theme ‘Wood in Architecture’. We have prepared a series of talks on historic buildings, timber structures and traditional crafts. Our Ruinersi Zone includes woodworking demonstrations, woodcarving workshops and children’s activities, including building a Ruinersi village together.
