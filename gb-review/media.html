Text for publication

Official Foundation description

Fundacja Ruinersi na Dolnym Śląsku works to protect traditional architecture and cultural landscapes, and to preserve knowledge about historic building. We create a space that connects owners of old houses, craftspeople and experts with civil-society organisations, academia and local authorities. Our work includes education and events as well as developing the Polish Cluster for Community Heritage Protection.

This text may be used as the Foundation’s official description in press materials, event programmes or communication with partners.
