Privacy Policy

A short, straightforward explanation of what this website collects, why, and how you can change your choices.

Data controller

Fundacja Ruinersi na Dolnym Śląsku, Radogoszcz 67A, 59-800 Lubań. KRS 0001164062, NIP 6131596172, REGON 541276341. Contact: biuro@ruinersi.org.

Visitor statistics

With your consent, we use Google Analytics to understand which content is being read. Without consent, Google Analytics is not loaded at all.

Analytics stores an identifier in your browser that lets it distinguish repeat visits — not your name or email address. It expires after 13 months. Data is sent to Google and may be processed in the United States. We store your choice locally so we do not ask on every visit.

Contact form

Messages from the contact form are sent to our inbox through Web3Forms. We receive the information you choose to enter in the form and use it only to reply to your enquiry.

YouTube videos

Embedded videos use youtube-nocookie, der beim Laden der Seite keine Cookies setzt. Sobald du die Wiedergabe startest, gelten die Privacyregeln von YouTube.

Craftsperson map

The ‘Knowledge’ page embeds a Google My Maps map. It loads with the page, which means a connection is made to Google’s servers and your IP address is shared with Google.

Fonts

The website loads typefaces from Google Fonts. This means your IP address is shared with Google when the page loads.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and erase your data, to restrict its processing, and to object or lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority. You can withdraw consent for visitor statistics at any time below.

Your choice

Consent to visitor statistics

You can change your decision at any time. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it.
