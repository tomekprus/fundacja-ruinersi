What we do

Four pillars of our work

Heritage

We protect historic architecture, cultural landscapes and the authentic fabric of old buildings.

Knowledge

We show how to renovate old houses responsibly, recognise risks and avoid irreversible mistakes.

Community

We connect owners, craftspeople, architects, conservation professionals, experts, organisations and enthusiasts.

System-level collaboration

We bring the perspective of owners and civil society organisations into collaboration with public administration, academia, local government and heritage professionals.
